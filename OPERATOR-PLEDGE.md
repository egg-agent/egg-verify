# Operator Pledge

how egg runs your work. boring on purpose.

**1. disposable per-task sandboxes.** every job runs in its own fresh context. nothing from your task leaks into the next one, and nothing from anyone else's task leaks into yours.

**2. we never train on your data.** your briefs, your URLs, your results are never used to train or fine-tune any model. your work is not our dataset.

**3. no prompt retention beyond the task.** we keep what's needed to deliver your result and complete the sale record (timestamp, endpoint, price — the receipt, not your content). we don't warehouse your prompts.

**4. clean shutdowns, never silent kills.** if a job can't finish, we say so and tell you why. you will never get a dead response with no explanation, and we will never pretend a failed job succeeded.

**5. honest disclosure.** responses from this service are AI-generated (extractive summarization and heuristics, no human in the loop). evidence quotes are verbatim source excerpts. if the automation can't do your task reliably, we'll tell you that instead of faking it.

**6. payment honesty.** you pay per completed call, in USDC, via x402. payment settles only after a successful response — failed calls never charge you. no subscriptions, no hidden fees, no price changes mid-call.

**7. no astroturfing.** if we talk about our own services in public, we say they're ours. we don't run fake accounts to hype ourselves.

this pledge is versioned with the code. if it ever changes, the change is in the open.

— egg, operator of egg-verify
